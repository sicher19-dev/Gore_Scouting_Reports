Game Plan

The whole start in six calls — four of them are answers to August 15.

Gauge

Read the first inning before trusting anything — the extra day was the club calling the pitch; the radar says if it landed. 95+ means the full plan: ladders everywhere they're drawn, aggressive zone-stealing, six-plus innings in play. Below 95 on five days' rest means the low-velo gear without ego — soft-heavy, ladders only to Muncy and Serven, edges for everyone else, and a shorter honest outing beats a longer stubborn one. Either way the addresses below are non-negotiable; velocity buys margin, it doesn't replace inches.

Bolte

The 3-for-3 gets answered with addresses, not adrenaline. Every Sacramento hit came where the card said don't: the one zone curve, the dead-middle fastball, the lazy up-in. Every out came where it said do: buried curves (59% whiff below — best on the team) and honest ladders (44%). Same hitter, same swing, same book — the rematch is purely an execution referendum, and it leads off four times.

License

Gelof's curve license gets its full audition in the 2-hole — the strongest freeze profile this series has ever graded. 43% whiff, 7% chase, .220 damage: open with it for called strikes, return to it with two, and let the ladder (41%) do the rest. The changeup ban is absolute (.441). If the license cashes three times, the top of the order becomes Bolte-plus-two-free-outs.

Menus

Three corrected menus from the reverse report: Butler, Serven, and the middle third. Butler: fastballs and fades, zero sliders (the nine-pitch wrong-menu walk does not repeat — his 50% changeup whiff was never used last time). Serven: the changeup stays below the thigh (his single was a letters-height gift). And the middle third is closed to everyone — Bolte 107.3, Williams 98.9 and Grichuk's 108.2 in Chicago are a five-start thread of heart-of-plate receipts that ends with inches, not hope.

Harvest

Seven-plus strikeouts are drawn in the same vertical geometry as last time — with two spin inversions honored. Muncy (51% up + the grown 37% slider door), Serven (60% up), Bolte (buried curves), Gelof (license + ladder), White and Hernaiz (46–62% soft chase). The inversions: no curves to Muncy (11% whiff — he takes and hits them) and none in the zone to McNeil. The whitelist rides at five clean starts; Muncy's .817 stays a museum piece.

Park

Globe Life, home edition: the roof forgives what Sutter Health punished — don't spend the refund. Heim's shelf double and Bolte's homer both fly less here, and the big center swallows the flare band that fell in Sacramento (three sub-82 EV hits that night). The bandbox tax is gone; the address standard isn't. Sixteen days ago this lineup needed four gifts to score three runs. Deliver zero gifts and the rematch reads like the trilogy's third act.
